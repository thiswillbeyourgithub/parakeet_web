# 13.0.1 (2026-11-23)

## UltiMed v1 is the medical model again

UltiMed v2 turned out to give often far worse results than v1, so v1 is the default medical model again: the medical dictation mode, old links and saved settings that named the unversioned UltiMed model now land on v1.

UltiMed v2 stays available, listed last and tagged "(experimental)". Picking it shows a warning, and the choice is not remembered, so the next visit goes back to the default model.

## Medical dictation

- The French medical boost list now applies at strength 2 instead of 1.
- The drug-name and medical-term corrections now come from version 2.0.3 of the rule list (45,112 rules).
